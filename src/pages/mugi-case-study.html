Overview

Give the mascot
somewhere to go.

MUGI started as a constraint: how do you make a meme-adjacent token feel considered without sanding off its joy? The answer is a complete world — one that feels collectible, legible and ready to travel.

RoleConcept / art direction / creative developmentScopeIdentity, web, motion, launch graphics

The cutest dog

Japanese packaging culture gave the system its structure. A toy mascot gave it a point of view. The result is warm, graphic and a little bit strange in the right way.
