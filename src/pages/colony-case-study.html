01

CONCEPT

ONE HOLDER.
ONE ANT.

COLONY converts a familiar crypto abstraction — token holders — into a spatial system. Every holder becomes one procedural ant, making distribution readable through density, movement and formation.

COLONY ONE HOLDER.

02

PROCEDURAL DESIGN

NO HERO
IMAGE.

The visual world is generated at runtime: terrain, ants, paths, clusters, particles and light. This avoids the alignment problems of compositing unrelated AI-generated images.

01 INSTANCED HOLDERS

Thousands of holders share a small set of geometries and materials.

02 PROCEDURAL TERRAIN

Geometry displacement creates a repeatable dark-earth world.

03 SCROLL CAMERA

The browser scroll position becomes a controlled cinematic camera path.

04 HOLDER INSPECTION

Raycasting maps rendered instances back to deterministic holder records.
